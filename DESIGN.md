- Preserve the existing dark studio surface, pink primary action, compact corners, and document-style ledger. Page shells stay fixed; role selection, detail, and ledger sections scroll internally. Avoid gradients, decorative motion, and a competing token palette.

Short opacity or transform transitions are permitted only when reduced motion is respected.
